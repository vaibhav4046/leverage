# LinkedIn virality research, 5 Sep 2026

Read: van der Blom's 2026 algorithm report coverage (1.3M posts, 50k creators), Justin Welsh's
anatomy of a viral post, Lara Acosta's SLAY framework, three carousel guides, ContentIn's
format strategy, Melanie Goodman's data-backed fixes, AuthoredUp's best-performing content.
YouTube transcripts were attempted (McTighe "1,000 hooks", Escobar "131 hooks") but the
transcript panel never rendered for the auto-dubbed video and caption endpoints returned
empty; the same creators' findings were taken from the written summaries instead.

## What decides reach now
- Dwell time and saves. 61+ s dwell = 15.6% engagement vs 1.2% at 0-3 s. Saves weigh 5x a like.
- Comments and saves weigh 3-5x likes. 20 thoughtful comments beat 200 reactions.
- First 90 minutes after posting decide most reach (was ~4 hours in 2024).
- One external link in the body: -18.8% reach. Put links in the first comment.
- No hashtags beats hashtags by 5-10%; 10+ hashtags: -30 to -50%.
- 2-4 posts a week is the sweet spot; daily posting: -26% per post.

## Format
- Document carousels: 1.45x reach, 2.5-3.1x engagement vs text, 1.8x saves, 3.4x reach vs single image.
- Single image: 0.8-1.2x reach. Vertical 4:5 gets +32%. Infographics and screenshots of real
  results earn saves; dense images without hierarchy do not.
- Video reach fell 72% in two years.
- Carousel rules: 8-12 slides, 30-50 words per slide, 1080x1350 PDF under 10 MB, cover =
  bold claim + specific number + curiosity gap, one idea per slide, slide numbers, one CTA on
  the last slide, minimum 24pt type, 2-3 colours, a face somewhere in the deck helps.

## Caption
- Hook under 210 characters; the first line is worth 95% of the post.
- Start with a result, a contradiction or a number. Never with context.
- 8-10 words per line, one idea per line, blank lines between.
- One call to action only. A question at the end earns comments.
- Caption for a carousel: 3-5 lines, a second hook above the cover, not a summary of it.
